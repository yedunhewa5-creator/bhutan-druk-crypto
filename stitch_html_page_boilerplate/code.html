Terms & Conditions

Draft v0.1 · Requires market-specific legal review before launch.

Service availability

Customer registration, verified balances, payment processing, order execution, escrow, and wallet services are not connected in this build. Currency selection alone does not mean a payment method or market is supported. Do not send funds or rely on an offer unless the relevant service is live and transaction details are shown for your confirmation.

P2P advertisements

A seller ad may be published only after an owner-verified available balance of at least 1,000 USDT is recorded. Ad quantity must not exceed that verified balance. This application currently has no customer identity service or automated balance ledger, so it cannot automatically enforce customer eligibility.

Wallet authorization

A connected wallet must remain under its user's control. The platform must never request or store a wallet seed phrase or private key. Each wallet transaction must be shown to the user and approved in the wallet by that user. Platform administration does not grant access to customer wallet keys or permission to move customer funds.

KYC, support, and disputes

KYC tracking and support cases are handled through the owner backend. Do not submit identity documents through the current support form. Trade dispute handling and customer account access are not connected.

Before publication

This draft does not define governing law, licensing, fees, refunds, privacy retention, market eligibility, or dispute venue. Complete and review those terms with qualified counsel for each market before accepting customers or funds.
